# Profile voice

Open **Administration → Profile → Voice → Speech synthesis provider**. App
settings also links to this editor. These choices affect the selected Hermes
profile; Android speech-engine and local voice choices remain in App settings.

Choose a provider and complete any required setup. When it is ready, choose a
voice from the compact selector. Selecting a voice saves it immediately. Use the
adjacent Play icon to hear a sample with the confirmed selection; Stop cancels
preparation or playback.

**Advanced** contains models, existing credentials, and custom voice IDs.
ElevenLabs offers voices from your account. Edge's list is labelled
**Suggested voices**; use **Advanced → Voice ID** for another voice. Other
providers offer custom IDs when supported, or let you preview their current
default. Nous Subscription and direct OpenAI are separate provider choices even
when they share voices.

Rapid selections settle on your latest choice, and Play waits for the save to
be confirmed. If a save cannot be confirmed, Wing retains the last confirmed
value and asks you to Refresh. Review it before retrying.

Refresh, another selection, leaving the editor, or backgrounding Wing stops
sample playback. A save already underway can finish after you leave. Changes
made from another client can affect the profile; refresh if the sample or
selection no longer matches what you expect.

See [Voice](VOICE.md) for using speech in conversations.
