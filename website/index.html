profiles and projects

Choose a profile or project

Tap a profile to focus its chats, or combine profiles in one view. Switch projects with the Project filter, then group, sort and choose the details you want to see. Wing remembers that setup for each connection.

Multitask with Recents

Spot new replies and work waiting for your input. From any chat or group discussion, swipe sideways with two fingers to switch, or pinch into a card stack and tap the one you want.

Manage your bots

Keep a conversation with each bot

Find bots across your saved connections, see their latest messages and return to each bot's continuing chat. Pin the bots you use most, or filter by Working and Needs input.

Bring bots into a shared discussion

Give 2–6 bots a shared purpose, choose who responds and follow member progress in one discussion. Extend it when more rounds are needed. Hermes coordinates accepted work on your server, so it can continue after Wing closes.

Need to finish a step yourself? Take control of the bot's desktop with your phone keyboard, touch gestures and clipboard, then hand it back with a note to continue.

Download for Android

Install a signed APK from GitHub Releases.
Choose arm64-v8a for most current phones.

What you need

Android 7.0 or newer and a Hermes server with its dashboard, Desktop Gateway and a configured model provider, reachable from your phone.

Use HTTPS or an encrypted private network for remote access. Your server and model providers may have their own costs.

Does work continue when my phone disconnects?

Work accepted by Hermes can continue when your phone disconnects. Reconnect to follow it; new messages and live controls need a connection.

Will I get notifications when I leave the app?

Wing can notify you about replies and input requests through live connections. Android background restrictions, force-stop and network loss can interrupt delivery. The notification guide explains setup and limits.

Can I connect with just a model-provider API key?

No. Wing needs a Hermes setup with its dashboard, Desktop Gateway and a configured model provider.
