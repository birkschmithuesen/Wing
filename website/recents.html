Find running work and chats waiting for you

Recents gathers chats with messages from the last 24 hours and ongoing work across profiles on your current connection. Cards show the latest message, work or input status and unread replies, ordered by message freshness. Older conversations remain in Chats.

Detached agents and processes can keep a chat visible as ongoing work even when its main turn has ended. This status shows what Hermes reports; live controls still depend on the chat's availability.

Tap a conversation to open it with its profile selected.

Switch chats with two fingers

From any chat or group discussion, swipe sideways with two fingers to move to the next or previous conversation. Diagonal swipes work too. The last chat links back to the first.

Pinch inward to open the card stack. Lift your fingers and it stays open. Swipe through the cards with one finger, then tap a card to expand that conversation.

Back closes the card stack first, then returns to Recents. Opening a chat directly from Chats, Bots or Groups keeps that list as its return destination.

Notice replies while you work elsewhere

A soft edge cue points toward another conversation with a new reply. Amber indicates a confirmed request for your input. You can check that chat without returning to the list, even while typing. Tool activity alone does not create an unread reply. Reading progress and your place in the conversation are tracked separately during the current app session.
