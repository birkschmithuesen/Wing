Your first chat
with Hermes

Connect Wing to your Hermes, then pick up the conversation on your phone.

Download Wing for Android

1. Install Wing

You need an Android 7.0+ phone and a working Hermes Cloud instance or your own Hermes server.

Download the latest APK and open it on your phone. Choose arm64-v8a for most phones. Allow installation when Android asks.

Need a Hermes server first? Start with the self-hosting guide.

2. Connect Hermes

Open Wing and tap Connect your agent. Choose Hermes Cloud to sign in with Nous Portal and select your instance, or Use an address for your own server.

Use an address that opens in your phone’s browser. Most setups can leave Custom setup alone.

3. Start a chat

Choose a profile in Chats, create a conversation and send your first message. Try “Give me three ideas for a quick lunch.”

Next, explore bots and group discussions or switch between conversations with Recents.

Need a hand?

The connection check failed

Open the dashboard address in your phone’s browser and check your network or VPN. Use your Hermes dashboard login, not a model-provider API key.

If profiles load but chat or history fails, use the current Hermes gateway Webapp address. A standalone dashboard can load profiles without supporting Wing's chat and history contract. The server setup guide covers the details.

Notifications, offline use and backups

Allow background battery use for screen-off notifications, and reopen Wing after restarting your phone. Android restrictions or network loss can interrupt delivery.

Hermes can continue accepted work while you’re offline. New messages need a connection; some recently loaded chats remain readable.

Configuration backups include connections and preferences, with optional encryption. Chats stay on Hermes; drafts and queues are excluded. Read the upgrade notes before updating.

Where does my data go?

Your chosen Hermes server and model providers process your content. The Wing maintainer does not relay your conversations. Connection details and preferences stay on your device.
