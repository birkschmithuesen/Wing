Return to each bot's conversation

Open Bots from the side menu to see agents across your saved Hermes connections. Each row shows the latest message from that bot's continuing chat. Tap the name or message to return to it.

Pin the bots you use most. Filter by Working or Needs input, or hide a bot from the roster. Back restores your tab, search and filter.

Name and configure your bots

Tap an avatar to change the bot's display name, shape, color or image. Upload an avatar or generate one through the profile's configured image provider. Appearance edits save as you make them.

Bot settings opens that bot's instructions, models, accounts, skills and tools. Create a new bot or duplicate an existing one to start with its configuration.

Saved bot avatars also appear beside their continuing chats in Chats, Recents and conversation titles. Hold a profile icon in the Chats header to open its Bot Chat directly.

Ask several bots in one discussion

Create a group with 2–6 bots from the same Hermes connection. Ask the whole group, or tap an @handle to address a particular member. Their replies appear together in the discussion.

Hermes runs the discussion on your server, so accepted work can continue when Wing goes into the background. Review approval requests, stop the work or retry an interrupted task from the group.

Open Shared settings to give the group a purpose and shared notes. Choose Everyone for all members, Lead for one member to select who replies, or Auto for an extra model call to choose recipients. Mentions address members directly.

Inspect member progress while they work. If a discussion reaches its round limit, Continue grants more rounds within Hermes' extension allowance. A stopped or exhausted discussion cannot continue.

Take over a bot's desktop

Choose View screen from a bot's menu to watch its desktop. With Hermes Bot Screen configured on the host, take control to finish a step yourself, then hand it back to the agent.

Tap to click, hold to right-click, pinch to zoom and use one finger to pan the zoomed view. Scroll with two fingers or the side strip. Open the phone keyboard to type, use special keys or paste clipboard text without pressing Enter.

When opened from a chat, the agent panel keeps its latest reply or pending request beside the desktop in landscape and below it in portrait. Open Conversation to read or queue a message without replacing your main draft. Answer approvals and private-input requests in the main chat.

Hand back control with an optional note asking the agent to continue. Control is returned before the note is sent; if sending fails, Wing keeps it for retry. A screen opened directly from the roster has no selected chat and simply returns control.
