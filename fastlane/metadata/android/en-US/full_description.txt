Your agent, with you

Wing brings your Hermes Agent to Android. Pick up a conversation, follow work in progress, and send the next idea wherever you are.

FIND YOUR FLOW
Browse chats across profiles and projects. Search, pin and filter conversations, watch replies stream in, and jump back to the right message or output.

STAY IN CONTROL
Steer work as it happens, queue a follow-up, review supported approvals, and see what subagents and goals are doing. Drafts and staged files stay with you through navigation and connection interruptions.

USE YOUR PHONE
Attach photos and files, share content from another app, dictate into an editable draft, and read replies aloud. Preview images, PDFs, Markdown, diagrams and supported media; save useful outputs through Android.

RUN YOUR HERMES SETUP
Give bot groups shared instructions, choose who responds and follow their progress. Manage profiles, model defaults, protected skills and MCP connectors. Schedule server-run work with a timezone and missed-run policy, deliver results to groups, or stop a run without disabling its schedule. Check health and diagnostics, explore usage and model trends, and choose a theme, accent color and text size that feel right.

Wing connects to a compatible Hermes dashboard and Desktop Gateway that you operate or have permission to use. It does not run a model on your phone. Android 7.0 or newer is required; local on-device dictation needs Android 12 or newer and a supported speech service. Your server and model provider may have their own costs.

For remote connections, use HTTPS or an encrypted private network. Wing's privacy policy and setup guide are available in the project repository; the policy is also available offline in App settings.
